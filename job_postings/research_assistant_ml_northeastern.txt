Title: Graduate Research Assistant, Applied Machine Learning
Company: Northeastern University, Institute for Experiential AI
Location: Boston, MA

Overview
Support faculty-led research projects applying machine learning to applied
domains, from data collection through publication.

Responsibilities
- Conduct structured literature reviews and synthesize findings.
- Build end-to-end data pipelines for research datasets, including OCR and
  text extraction from source documents.
- Implement and compare models across supervised and unsupervised methods.
- Apply similarity measures and evaluation metrics appropriate to each study.
- Maintain reproducible, well-documented experimental workflows.
- Co-author papers and present at internal seminars.

Qualifications
- Enrolled in a Master's or PhD program in a quantitative field.
- Python, scikit-learn, PyTorch.
- Experience with research workflows: documentation, version control,
  reproducibility.
- Strong written communication.

Preferred
- Prior research internship experience.
- Exposure to generative AI architectures and transformer models.
