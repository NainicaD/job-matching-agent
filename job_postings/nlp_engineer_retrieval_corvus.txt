Title: NLP Engineer, Retrieval & Generation
Company: Corvus AI
Location: Remote (US)

The role
Build retrieval-augmented generation systems: semantic search, embedding
pipelines, and reranking over large document collections.

Responsibilities
- Design and evaluate retrieval pipelines: chunking strategies, embedding
  models, vector stores, and hybrid lexical/semantic search.
- Fine-tune and evaluate transformer encoders for domain-specific retrieval.
- Build evaluation harnesses measuring recall@k, MRR, and end-to-end answer
  quality.
- Apply OCR and document parsing to bring scanned sources into the index.
- Work with LLM APIs for the generation stage, including prompt design and
  structured output validation.

Requirements
- Strong Python; experience with PyTorch and HuggingFace Transformers.
- Hands-on experience with embeddings, similarity search, and TF-IDF or
  BM25 baselines.
- Familiarity with vector databases such as Chroma, FAISS, or Pinecone.
- Experience evaluating generative systems, not just building them.

Nice to have
- Experience with GANs or other generative architectures.
- Published research or a strong portfolio of applied NLP projects.
