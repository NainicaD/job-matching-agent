Title: Quantitative Research Analyst
Company: Tessera Capital
Location: Boston, MA

Overview
Research and validate systematic strategies using statistical and machine
learning methods on financial and alternative datasets.

Responsibilities
- Design and test hypotheses on large panel datasets.
- Apply causal inference methods to separate correlation from effect.
- Build gradient-boosted and regularized linear models; rigorously guard
  against overfitting with cross-validation and out-of-sample testing.
- Construct reproducible research pipelines in Python.
- Document methodology and present to the investment committee.

Required
- Master's or PhD in Statistics, Economics, Data Science, or related.
- Strong statistical foundations: hypothesis testing, regression,
  regularization, cross-validation.
- Python with pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn.
- SQL for large dataset manipulation.

Preferred
- Experience with causal inference: difference-in-differences,
  instrumental variables, propensity matching.
- Interest in fraud or anomaly detection methods.
