Title: Machine Learning Engineer, Intern (Summer 2026)
Company: Lumen Analytics
Location: Boston, MA

About the role
Join the Recommendation & Ranking team to prototype models, run offline
evaluations, and ship improvements to systems serving millions of users.

What you will do
- Build and evaluate ranking and retrieval models: collaborative filtering,
  two-tower retrieval, gradient-boosted trees, transformer-based rerankers.
- Design offline experiments: define metrics, build evaluation pipelines,
  and analyze results with statistical rigor.
- Construct data pipelines transforming raw event logs into model-ready
  training datasets using SQL and Python.
- Communicate findings to technical and non-technical partners.

Minimum qualifications
- Pursuing a Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Data Science, or Statistics.
- Strong Python programming skills.
- Solid machine learning fundamentals: supervised learning, model evaluation,
  regularization, cross-validation.
- Experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Working knowledge of SQL, pandas, and NumPy.

Preferred qualifications
- Recommender systems: matrix factorization, collaborative filtering,
  content-based filtering, or learning-to-rank.
- Transformer architectures and modern NLP or information retrieval.
- Model interpretability techniques such as SHAP or attention analysis.
- Exposure to A/B testing and causal inference.
