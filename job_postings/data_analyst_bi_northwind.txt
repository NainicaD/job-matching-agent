Title: Data Analyst, Business Intelligence
Company: Northwind Retail Group
Location: Quincy, MA

Summary
Own reporting and dashboards for the merchandising organization, turning
transactional data into decisions.

Responsibilities
- Write and optimize SQL queries against the enterprise data warehouse.
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI and Tableau.
- Produce recurring reports on compliance metrics, inventory, and sales
  performance for leadership.
- Validate and transform data; document definitions and data lineage.
- Partner with stakeholders across departments to scope reporting needs.

Required qualifications
- Bachelor's degree and two years of analytics experience.
- Strong SQL and advanced Excel skills.
- Experience with a BI tool: Power BI, Tableau, or Looker.
- Clear written communication for non-technical audiences.

Preferred
- Python or R for data manipulation.
- Experience with Smartsheet or similar project tracking tools.
- Background supporting enterprise systems such as SAP.
