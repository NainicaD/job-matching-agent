Title: SAP GTS Consultant
Company: Arbor Consulting Partners
Location: Hybrid - Boston, MA

Role
Implement and support SAP Global Trade Services for multinational clients
across export and import compliance.

Responsibilities
- Configure end-to-end GTS solutions: export controls, import controls,
  sanctioned party list screening, and preference management.
- Lead requirements workshops with clients and stakeholders; document
  business needs and translate them into system configuration.
- Manage customs and compliance workflows for global trade processes.
- Support integrations and broker setups between SAP and external systems.
- Drive PMO coordination: track tasks, timelines, and deliverables.
- Produce dashboards on compliance metrics for client leadership.

Required
- 2+ years hands-on SAP GTS implementation experience.
- Experience across the full project lifecycle from requirements through
  design, configuration, testing, and production deployment.
- Strong client-facing communication skills.
- Proficiency with Excel and project tracking tools such as Smartsheet.

Preferred
- Exposure to SAP Business AI components and workflow automation.
- Interest in applying analytics or AI to compliance reporting.
