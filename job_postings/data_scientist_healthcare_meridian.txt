Title: Data Scientist, Clinical Analytics
Company: Meridian Health Systems
Location: Cambridge, MA

Position overview
Build predictive models on large-scale claims and clinical data to support
utilization management and clinical decision support.

Responsibilities
- Develop and validate supervised learning models for claim denial prediction
  and patient risk stratification on large healthcare datasets.
- Engineer features from structured claims data and unstructured clinical text.
- Apply explainability methods (SHAP, feature importance) so clinical
  stakeholders can interpret and trust model output.
- Build reproducible pipelines from raw ingestion through model evaluation.
- Present findings to clinical and operational leadership.

Required
- Master's degree in Data Science, Statistics, Biostatistics, or related field.
- Python, SQL, scikit-learn, pandas.
- Experience with gradient-boosted classifiers (XGBoost, LightGBM).
- Understanding of model evaluation under class imbalance: AUROC, F1,
  precision-recall tradeoffs.

Preferred
- Experience with clinical NLP or transformer models such as ClinicalBERT.
- Familiarity with causal inference for observational healthcare data.
- Exposure to HIPAA-regulated data environments.
