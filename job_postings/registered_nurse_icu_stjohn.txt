Title: Registered Nurse, Intensive Care Unit
Company: St. John Regional Medical Center
Location: Springfield, MA

Position
Provide direct nursing care to critically ill patients in a 24-bed ICU.

Responsibilities
- Assess, plan, implement, and evaluate patient care plans.
- Administer medications and titrate vasoactive drips per protocol.
- Monitor hemodynamic status; manage ventilated patients.
- Respond to codes and participate in rapid response teams.
- Educate patients and families on care plans and discharge instructions.
- Document care accurately in the electronic health record.

Required
- Active RN license in Massachusetts.
- BSN preferred; ADN considered with experience.
- BLS and ACLS certification.
- Two years of acute care nursing experience, ICU preferred.
- CCRN certification a plus.

Schedule
Three 12-hour night shifts per week, rotating weekends and holidays.
