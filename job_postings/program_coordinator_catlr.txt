Title: Program Coordinator, Center for Advancing Teaching and Learning
Company: Northeastern University
Location: Boston, MA

Position summary
Support day-to-day operations of the Center: coordinate programming, manage
event logistics, maintain records, and serve as first point of contact.

Responsibilities
- Coordinate scheduling, logistics, and communications for workshops,
  seminars, and the annual conference.
- Maintain program records and databases; produce participation reports for
  leadership.
- Serve as primary point of contact for faculty, staff, and student
  participants; resolve scheduling conflicts.
- Draft and edit web content, newsletters, and announcements.
- Support budget tracking, purchasing, and reimbursement processing.
- Coordinate across departments and external partners.

Required
- Bachelor's degree and two years of administrative or program coordination
  experience in an academic or nonprofit setting.
- Excellent written and verbal communication, including writing for
  non-technical audiences.
- Strong organizational skills managing concurrent projects and deadlines.
- Proficiency with Microsoft Office, spreadsheets, and calendar management.

Preferred
- Higher education administration experience.
- Reporting tools: Excel, Smartsheet, Power BI, or Tableau.
- Experience supporting research programs or teaching initiatives.
