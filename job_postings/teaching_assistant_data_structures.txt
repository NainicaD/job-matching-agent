Title: Teaching Assistant, Data Structures and Machine Learning
Company: Vasavi College of Engineering
Location: Hyderabad, India

Role

Machine Learning & AI courses.

  fundamentals.
- Prior experience explaining technical material to students.
- Reliability and organization in managing coursework logistics.

Preferred
- Experience coordinating course operations across multiple semesters.
- Python and algorithm implementation experience.
