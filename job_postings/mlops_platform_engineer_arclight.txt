Title: MLOps Platform Engineer
Company: Arclight Infrastructure
Location: Remote (US)

The role
Own the platform that trains, serves, and monitors models in production.

Responsibilities
- Build and operate model serving infrastructure on Kubernetes with
  autoscaling, canary rollouts, and blue/green deploys.
- Manage infrastructure as code with Terraform across multi-region AWS.
- Own CI/CD pipelines for model artifacts; implement automated rollback.
- Instrument production monitoring: latency SLOs, drift detection, alerting
  via Prometheus and Grafana.
- Manage feature store infrastructure and streaming ingestion with Kafka.
- Harden container images and manage secrets rotation.

Required
- 4+ years in platform, infrastructure, or SRE roles.
- Deep Kubernetes and Terraform experience.
- Strong Go or Python for tooling.
- Production on-call experience owning uptime.

Preferred
- Experience with Ray, Kubeflow, or Airflow at scale.
- gRPC service design and load testing.
