Program Coordinator, Center for Teaching and Learning
Full-time, on-campus

Position summary
The Program Coordinator supports the day-to-day operations of the Center,
coordinating programming, managing event logistics, maintaining records, and
serving as a first point of contact for faculty and student participants.

Responsibilities
- Coordinate scheduling, logistics, and communications for workshops,
  seminars, and the annual conference.
- Maintain program records and databases; produce regular reports on
  participation and outcomes for leadership.
- Serve as the primary point of contact for faculty, staff, and student
  participants; respond to inquiries and resolve scheduling conflicts.
- Draft and edit web content, newsletters, and announcements.
- Support budget tracking, purchasing, and reimbursement processing.
- Coordinate across multiple departments and external partners.

Required qualifications
- Bachelor's degree and two years of administrative or program coordination
  experience in an academic or nonprofit setting.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including writing for
  non-technical audiences.
- Strong organizational skills and demonstrated attention to detail while
  managing multiple concurrent projects and deadlines.
- Proficiency with Microsoft Office, spreadsheets, and calendar management.
- Experience maintaining records and producing summary reports.

Preferred qualifications
- Experience in higher education administration.
- Familiarity with event management and stakeholder communication.
- Experience with reporting or dashboard tools such as Excel, Smartsheet,
  Power BI, or Tableau.
- Experience supporting research programs or teaching initiatives.
