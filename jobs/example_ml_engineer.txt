Machine Learning Engineer, Intern (Summer 2026)
Recommendation & Ranking Team

About the role
We are looking for an ML engineering intern to work on large-scale
recommendation and ranking systems. You will work alongside research
scientists and production engineers to prototype models, run offline
evaluations, and ship improvements to systems serving millions of users.

What you will do
- Build and evaluate ranking and retrieval models (collaborative filtering,
  two-tower retrieval, gradient-boosted trees, transformer-based rerankers).
- Design and run offline experiments: define metrics, build evaluation
  pipelines, and analyze results with statistical rigor.
- Construct data pipelines to transform raw event logs into model-ready
  training datasets using SQL and Python.
- Collaborate with engineers to move promising prototypes toward production,
  including model serving and monitoring.
- Communicate findings clearly to both technical and non-technical partners.

Minimum qualifications
- Currently pursuing a Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Data Science,
  Statistics, or a related quantitative field.
- Strong programming skills in Python.
- Solid grounding in machine learning fundamentals: supervised learning,
  model evaluation, overfitting, regularization, and cross-validation.
- Experience with at least one deep learning framework (PyTorch or TensorFlow).
- Working knowledge of SQL and data manipulation libraries (pandas, NumPy).

Preferred qualifications
- Hands-on experience with recommender systems: matrix factorization,
  collaborative filtering, content-based filtering, or learning-to-rank.
- Experience with transformer architectures and modern NLP or information
  retrieval methods, including retrieval-augmented generation.
- Familiarity with model interpretability and explainability techniques
  such as SHAP or attention analysis.
- Experience with distributed data processing (Spark) or cloud platforms
  (AWS, GCP).
- Exposure to A/B testing and causal inference.
- Prior internship or research experience in an applied ML setting.
